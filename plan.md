## Visual direction

- **Design movement:** paper-cut punk zine meets a 2002 doll-beauty catalog, with gothic-grunge edge and liquid-metal jewelry motifs.
- **Core principles:** handmade texture over polished gradients; bold-but-readable editorial hierarchy; physical collage depth with chaos kept inside a clear tap-safe grid; shameless, playful confidence without pretending demo content is verified.
- **Color philosophy:** photocopy cream `#F4EFE6` is the paper stock; black `#0A0A0A` grounds type and outlines; hot pink `#FF1493` is the ownable Brat signal; burnt orange `#CC5500` and baby pink `#FFB6D9` add heat; chrome gradients between `#E8E8EE`, `#8A8A99`, and `#FFFFFF` contrast with the supplied logo’s black ink on transparent grounds. Maintain contrast for text and focus states.
- **Layout paradigm:** overlapping torn-paper panels, offset editorial columns and pasted photo strips; keep product filtering and checkout controls orderly and mobile-readable rather than making functional catalog controls chaotic.
- **Signature elements:** supplied crowned B/H monogram with heart; thick white sticker keylines with black drop-shadows; taped Polaroid/photo cards and small chrome-heart/star accents.
- **Interaction philosophy:** every control gives immediate tactile feedback; focus and pressed states remain clear; playful motion never delays checkout, blocks content, or depends on hover alone.
- **Animation:** brief sticker wiggle/peel on pointer hover and press; a restrained, one-time paper-slap reveal; subtle, low-distance desktop hero parallax; a small decorative heart/sparkle pointer trail and add-to-cart heart burst. Disable nonessential effects for `prefers-reduced-motion`; skip the pointer trail on touch, cap its work, and preserve keyboard/touch parity.
- **Typography:** supplied logo image for the actual wordmark; `Bangers` or `Titan One` for brief shouty cut-outs; `Space Mono` for useful labels/specs; a clean sans-serif fallback for paragraphs and forms. Use slight rotations only on display cut-outs.
- **Brand essence:** “Hair that takes the room before you do”—for baddies who want wigs with a loud, tactile Y2K point of view. Personality: **bratty, glam, rebellious**.
- **Brand voice:** short, confident, playful, a little sassy. Example headlines: “WIGS THAT HIT DIFFERENT.” and “YOU’RE OFFICIALLY BRATTY.” Example microcopy: “Slay on arrival.” and “Your crown, delivered.”
- **Wordmark/logo:** use the user-supplied black-line crown, heart, and B/H monogram artwork as the primary logo at every logo placement; do not redraw it as plain default-font text. Provide an accessible text name.
- **Signature brand color:** hot pink `#FF1493`, paired with the supplied logo’s crisp black linework on transparent backgrounds.

## Implementation structure

- `client/src/pages/`: route screens and route-specific copy for the home, wig catalog, product, lookbook, story, support, checkout and confirmation pages; the retired bundles path redirects to the wig catalog.
- `client/src/components/`: shared site shell, navigation, wig product cards, taped-receipt cart drawer, filters, sticker UI, FAQ helper, and footer.
- `client/src/styles/brathouse.css`: reusable design tokens, type scale, sticker buttons, torn-edge/taped cards, decorative elements, responsive styles, reduced-motion rules and add-to-cart heart burst.
- `client/src/contexts/` and `client/src/hooks/`: persistent cart/wishlist/promo state, decorative pointer effects, and per-route SEO metadata.
- `shared/catalog.ts`, `shared/money.ts`, `shared/seo.ts`, and `shared/demoContent.ts`: the exact user-provided wig SKUs/pricing, ZAR formatting, route metadata, and policy/copy data shared by UI and server.
- `server/db.ts`: managed database access; no bundle quote, checkout or payment-session handler is active until the owner completes supported payment setup.
- `drizzle/schema.ts` and the additive migration: existing schema retained as-is to preserve historical data; no new tables are needed for the product-only storefront.
- `client/public/manus-routes.json`: complete page-route manifest aligned with Wouter routes; the supplied logo is a local WebP and campaign visuals use managed `/manus-storage/async-images/…` URLs.
