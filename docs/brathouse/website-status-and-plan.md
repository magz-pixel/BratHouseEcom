## Decisions so far
- Two ways to buy: **Ready to ship** (the 10-piece South Africa sample drop) and **Made to order** (anything designed in the Build Your Wig studio, made on the factory line in Xuchang).
- Ordering is WhatsApp-first until a South African payment gateway is connected. Custom builds get quoted on WhatsApp, then paid.
- The custom price formula reproduces all 10 listed SKU prices exactly. Every other option price is an estimate until the factory confirms it.
- The site was moved off Manus-hosted photos. Drawn wig art stands in until the samples are photographed.

## Open inputs (blocking launch)
