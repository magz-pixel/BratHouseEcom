# BRATHOUSE wig shoot — camera guide

**Subject:** Wig shoot tomorrow — what we need from the camera

Hey,

Thanks for shooting tomorrow. These photos aren't only for the product pages. We'll use them to build a 360° spin of each wig, try 3D versions, and power the "see it on me" try-on in the builder. That's why some of the asks below are strict: same camera spot, same light, same background, every wig. Consistency matters more than fancy.

## 1. Setup (do once, then don't move anything)

- **Background:** plain, matte and seamless. Light grey or off-white paper or a sheet, with no creases. Keep it at least 1 m behind the mannequin so it blurs out and doesn't cast a shadow.
- **Light:** soft and even. Use two lights at 45° either side, or a big window plus a white board (foam board or sheet) bouncing light into the shadow side. No direct flash and no harsh sun. Turn off overhead lights so the colours don't mix.
- **Camera:** on a tripod at the mannequin's eye level, about 1.5–2 m away. Zoom in a little rather than going close; this avoids distortion. Mark the tripod's feet with tape.
- **Mannequin:** in the same spot for every wig, ideally on a lazy Susan or turntable so you can rotate it. Mark "front" on the base with tape. Put tape marks every 15° around the base (24 marks) or every 45° (8 marks) as a minimum.
- **Settings:** phone in the main 1× lens or a camera at about 50mm. Lock focus and exposure (long-press on iPhone or Android). No portrait mode, no beauty filters, no HDR "auto-enhance". Shoot in the highest resolution, and in RAW too if you can.
- **Colour check:** at the start, take one photo of a white sheet of paper in the set. It lets us correct the colour afterwards.

## 2. Before the photos, every wig

- Brush and style it the way a customer would wear it. Lay the lace flat and tuck any combs and straps.
- Centre the part (or style it the way it's sold).
- Use a lint roller on stray hairs and wipe dust off the mannequin.
- Take one **"bald" shot of the empty mannequin** at the start of the day from the front and both sides. We use this for the try-on cutouts.

## 3. Photos per wig (the must-haves)

Shoot each one landscape and portrait unless marked otherwise.

|---|---|---|
| 01 | Front, straight on | Main product photo. Whole wig in frame plus a bit of the neck. |
| 02 | Three-quarter left (45°) | |
| 03 | Left profile (90°) | |
| 04 | Back (180°) | Shows length and how it falls. |
| 05 | Right profile (270°) | |
| 06 | Three-quarter right (315°) | |
| 07 | Hairline close-up | Fill the frame with the lace and baby hairs at the front. Shows how real the hairline looks. |
| 08 | Part close-up | From above, looking down the part. |
| 09 | Texture close-up | Mid-length, in focus, so you can see the curl or wave pattern. |
| 10 | Ends close-up | Shows how full the ends are. |
| 11 | Inside the cap | Wig off the head, turned inside out on a flat surface: combs, straps and the lace size. |
| 12 | Length proof | Wig on the mannequin next to a tape measure from the crown down. |
| 13 | Hand-in-hair | A hand lifting or running through the hair. Shows movement and softness. |

**360° spin (the important one for the 3D view):** don't touch the camera. Rotate the mannequin one tape mark at a time and take a photo each time: 24 photos at 15°, or 36 at 10° if you have time. Start and end at front. Same zoom, same focus, nothing else changes.

## 4. Short videos per wig (vertical 9:16, 4K if possible, 5–15 s each)

1. **Slow turntable:** one full rotation over about 10 seconds, camera locked. This is also our backup for building 3D.
2. **Hand-in-hair / shake:** run fingers through it or give a gentle shake. Shows movement and bounce.
3. **Hairline close-up push-in:** move slowly from the whole head to the front lace.
4. **Brush-through:** one or two slow brush strokes. Satisfying and good for TikTok.
5. *(Optional)* **Handheld walk-around:** go slowly 360° around the mannequin at the same height, about 30 seconds and smooth. We'll try a 3D capture from it.

Keep the camera steady, and turn off stabilisation "crop" modes and filters. Natural sound is fine; we'll replace it.

Inside each folder:

- Photos: `<folder>_<shot#>_<name>.jpg`, for example `05-body-black-20_01_front.jpg` or `05-body-black-20_07_hairline.jpg`
- Spin: a `spin/` subfolder with `000.jpg, 015.jpg, 030.jpg … 345.jpg` (the number is the angle)
- Videos: `<folder>_vid_turntable.mp4`, `_vid_hand.mp4`, `_vid_hairline.mp4`, `_vid_brush.mp4`, `_vid_walkaround.mp4`

**Tip:** before each new wig, take a photo of a sheet of paper with the folder name written on it. If the naming slips, we can still sort them.

**Keep the originals.** Don't edit, crop or compress anything. Upload the full-size files (AirDrop, Google Drive or a USB stick, not WhatsApp, which compresses them).
